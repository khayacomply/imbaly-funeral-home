About Our Craft

Timeless Craftsmanship Under Mr Dlamini D

At Eternal Stone Memorials, under the dedicated leadership of Mr Dlamini D, every monument we create is a tribute to a life well-lived. Our master artisans combine traditional stone-carving techniques with modern precision to deliver memorials that endure for generations.

From simple headstones to elaborate family mausoleums, we work closely with each family in Mpumalanga and beyond to design a memorial that truly reflects the spirit of their loved one.
