Our Story

About Eternal Stone

A Legacy Carved in Stone

Founded in 1985 by master stonemason Robert Alden, Eternal Stone Memorials began as a small family workshop with a single mission: to create memorials worthy of the lives they honor.

Today, three generations later, we continue that tradition. Our team of artisans combines time-honored carving techniques with modern technology — from CNC precision to hand-finished detailing — to produce monuments of unmatched quality.

We believe a memorial is more than stone. It is a promise — that love, memory, and legacy will endure long after we are gone.
