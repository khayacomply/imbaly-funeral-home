Our Story

About Imbaly Funeral Home

A Legacy of Care and Craftsmanship

Registered in 2018 (Reg No: 2018/419812/07) and led by Mr Dlamini D, Imbaly Funeral Home began with a single, clear mission: to provide dignified, handcrafted memorials and compassionate funeral services worthy of the lives they honor, right here in the heart of Mpumalanga.

Today, we continue that proud tradition. Our dedicated team combines time-honored stone-carving techniques with modern precision to produce monuments of unmatched quality. We understand the cultural and personal significance of a final resting place, and we treat every project with the utmost respect.

We believe a memorial is more than just stone, and a funeral is more than a service. It is a lasting promise — that love, memory, and legacy will endure for generations to come.

The Process

How We Work

01

Consultation

We meet with your family (in person or via WhatsApp) to understand your vision, preferences, and budget.

02

Design

Our team creates detailed sketches and digital mockups for your review and approval before any work begins.

03

Crafting

Our skilled artisans bring the design to life using premium stone, expert engraving, and careful finishing.

04

Installation

We handle the safe delivery and professional placement at the cemetery of your choice across Mpumalanga.
